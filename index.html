Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our domestic, ironing, and bond cleaning services in Brisbane.

What areas across Brisbane do you service?

We service all Brisbane suburbs including the CBD, inner city (New Farm, Paddington, West End), northern suburbs (Chermside, Aspley, Nundah), southern suburbs (Carindale, Mount Gravatt, Sunnybank), and western suburbs (Toowong, Indooroopilly), as well as Ipswich & Logan.

What is included in your bond cleaning service?

Our bond cleaning is a top-to-bottom clean designed to secure your full bond return. It includes oven degreasing, internal/external cupboards, window sills and glass, wall spot cleaning, skirting boards, doors, ceiling fans, full bathroom descale and disinfection, and vacuuming/mopping.

Do I need to supply cleaning products or equipment?

No, our cleaners arrive fully equipped with hospital-grade, eco-friendly cleaning supplies and professional vacuum and mopping tools. If you have specific specialty surfaces or preferred supplies, we are happy to use them upon request.

How does the laundry and ironing service work?

We provide full wash, dry, and fold service ($38 per large load) and crisp professional ironing ($38/hour). You can schedule laundry alongside your domestic clean or book it as a standalone service.

Are your cleaners police-checked and vetted?

Yes. Every member of our team is police-checked, verified, experienced, and local to Brisbane. We take pride in building long-term trust with our clients.
